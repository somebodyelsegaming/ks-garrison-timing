Kingshot - Timing Your Reinforcements

When to send your march (reinforcement or solo) so it lands right before or right after an enemy rally hits.
Built by Somebody Else, the gnome you know.

Walk times (seconds, as the game shows them)

Your target

1. Their rally countdown 2. Their march 3. Their hit

How it works

enemy hits  = rally countdown + enemy walk time
I land      = when I send + my walk time

send when the rally countdown shows:
  land before:  my walk − enemy walk + gap
  land after:   my walk − enemy walk − gap

If that is below 0:00, the rally has already marched. Send when the
enemy march shows this many seconds left:
  land before:  my walk + gap
  land after:   my walk − gap

Walk times change with pets and buffs. Read yours in game with the same buffs you will have on. Whole-second times can be off by up to a second each, so a gap of 2-3 seconds is safer than 0.
